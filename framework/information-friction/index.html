FRAMEWORK

Information Friction

Information Friction is a framework within Narrative Engineering that examines the resistance affecting the flow, interpretation and transmission of narrative information.

Official Article Corpus GitHub

Description

Information Friction describes the factors that slow, distort or redirect the transmission of narrative information between a text and its audience. The framework provides a way to analyze informational resistance within stories and narrative systems.
