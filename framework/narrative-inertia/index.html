FRAMEWORK

Narrative Inertia

Narrative Inertia is a theoretical framework within Narrative Engineering that examines how established narrative structures continue to influence subsequent events even after the original causes have disappeared.

Official Website Corpus GitHub

Description

Narrative Inertia explains the persistence of narrative momentum. Once a story establishes a direction, expectations, or structural pattern, those elements tend to continue influencing interpretation and progression unless a sufficiently strong disruptive force alters them.
