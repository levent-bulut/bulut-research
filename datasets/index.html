Objective Projection Dataset

The Bulut Doctrine Narrative Engineering Corpus

Annotated dataset of 500 narrative scenes labelled for the six golden rules of the Objective Projection methodology. Includes a rule-based bilingual (Turkish–English) detection pipeline and hard-negative examples, designed for training and evaluating models that distinguish physics-based narrative construction from emotion-label narration.
